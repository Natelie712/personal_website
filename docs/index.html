Research

My current work sits at the intersection of representation geometry, large language models, and AI alignment.

I am interested in when the internal spaces of neural networks are curved, distorted, or otherwise resistant to simple linear descriptions—and what that means for reliably steering model behavior.

Current focus · geometry-aware intervention in LLMs

Curveball Steering: The Right Direction To Steer Isn’t Always Linear

Shivam Raval*, Hae Jin Song, Linlin Wu*, Abir Harrasse, Jeff M. Phillips, Fazl Barez, Amirali Abdullah * Co-first authors

We examine why linear steering directions can be unreliable in LLMs. By measuring geometric distortion in activation spaces, we motivate Curveball steering: a nonlinear method that operates in a polynomial-kernel feature space and better respects the model’s learned geometry.
